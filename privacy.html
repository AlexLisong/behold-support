Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Behold for iPhone is developed by Li Song. It records and combines camera video on your device. No account is required.

What stays on your device

Camera frames, microphone audio, previews, compositing and video encoding are processed on your iPhone. Recordings are saved to Photos with your permission, or to Behold’s local Files folder. Your layout and recording preferences are stored locally.

Data collection and tracking

Behold does not send recordings, camera frames, audio, personal information or usage analytics to the developer. It has no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not sell your data.

Permissions

  • Camera: captures the front and back views for preview and recording.
  • Microphone: records sound with your videos.
  • Add to Photos: saves recordings to your library. Behold does not request access to read your photo library.

You can manage permissions in iOS Settings. Without camera access recording is unavailable; without microphone access videos may have no sound. If Photos access is unavailable, the combined video is kept in Behold’s Files folder.

Your choices

You control your recordings and may export or delete them in Photos or Files. Copies exported to other apps, or synced by your own iCloud settings, are governed by those services. Deleting Behold removes its local app data; videos already saved to Photos remain until you delete them there.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages; requests to it are subject to GitHub’s privacy statement. The app opens these pages only when you select a support or privacy link.

Children

Behold does not collect personal information from children or require user accounts.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact info@xdreamers.com.
