Behold records your iPhone’s front and back cameras together for everyday videos, travel and vlogs. Choose picture in picture, top/bottom, or side-by-side layouts.

Record a video

  1. Open Behold and allow camera and microphone access.
  2. Choose a layout. Tap the swap button to change which camera is the main view.
  3. Tap the red record button. Use pause to take a break within the same clip, then tap stop to finish.
  4. Your combined video is saved to Photos when you grant permission.

Find your recordings

Combined videos appear in Photos. With raw clips enabled in Settings, the original front and back videos are saved in Files → On My iPhone → Behold → Raw Clips, or Photos if selected. If Photos permission is denied, Behold keeps the combined video in its Files folder.

Customize the view

Drag the picture-in-picture inset to a corner, or tap it to swap cameras. Settings includes a circle or rounded inset, three inset sizes, a border, vertical or horizontal video, stabilization, a countdown, and a mirrored front camera.

Compatibility

Requires iOS 17 or later and an iPhone that supports simultaneous front/back camera capture. Camera combinations, zoom and 60 fps availability depend on the device. Behold falls back to 30 fps when needed. Some models do not support dual-camera recording.

Troubleshooting

No camera preview: check Settings → Apps → Behold → Camera. If your iPhone cannot run both cameras together, Behold displays an explanation.

No sound: allow microphone access and check the microphone name shown in the app. A connected wired, USB or Bluetooth microphone may be selected by iOS.

Delayed preview: choose Standard or Off stabilization. Cinematic stabilization adds a noticeable preview delay.

Phone getting hot: stop recording and let it cool. Use 30 fps to reduce load.
